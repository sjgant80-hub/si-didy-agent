THE HEADLINE WIN
  Treat the FB Page as a CREDIBILITY SURFACE + a REELS DISTRIBUTION ENDPOINT,
  not a feed-engagement engine. New Pages get ~1-2% organic reach to followers
  and effectively ZERO to non-followers from static posts. Reels (9:16) bypass
  the follower graph and are the only reliable free distribution for a 0-follower
  Page in 2026.

  Operational translation for si-didy:
    · Ship one ~30-60s vertical Reel daily-ish showing the AI doing something real
    · Invite every post-engager to follow within 24h (Meta-native button)
    · 3-5 posts/week total · NOT daily-everywhere · Reels-weighted (~70/20/10)
    · Caption ≤ 80 chars · link in 1st comment · NEVER in body

CONFIRMED PLAYS (use these · ≥2/3 panel votes)
  1. Reels-first · every video distributes as a Reel in 2026 · 9:16 vertical only
  2. Invite post-engagers to follow within 24h · throttle 1 per 2.1s · cap 100/day
  3. Post 3-5x/week · NOT daily · peak engagement Pages cluster at the low end
  4. Reply to every comment within ~60 min during first 6h (MSI signal)
  5. Run a paired FB Group alongside the Page · Page = storefront · Group = build-in-public
  6. Use an INTERACTIVE free tool (FallMap-style) as primary lead magnet · NOT a PDF
     · gate the SAVE/EXPORT not the USE
  7. Messenger as structured intake (name, use-case, link) · NOT a bot persona
  8. If running ads: lean conversion-objective at $10-15/day · NOT Boost Post
     · only Boost a post that ALREADY over-indexed organically
  9. Show the build · 20-30s "AI doing one specific thing" Reels beat abstract tips
 10. Cross-post from LinkedIn/X with a 24h DELAY · NOT simultaneous
 11. Caption ≤ 80 chars above the fold · link in 1st comment · NEVER body

REFUTED COMMON WISDOM (do NOT do · ≥2/3 panel votes refute)
  ✗ "Reels get 22% more engagement · video reaches 135% more users" · recycled 2022-24
    vendor decks · directionally true but precision is theatre
  ✗ "Click-to-Messenger ads convert 3-5x better than landing pages" · all citations

  ✗ "70% of B2B buyers research on Facebook" · conflates "has account" with "researches B2B"

  ✗ "AI checklists convert 11.4% vs PDFs 4.7%" · suspicious precision · single vendor

  ✗ "Posting >1/day tanks engagement" · confounds rate with volume · misleading
  ✗ "New Pages need a 7-day warm-up or get spam-flagged" · no Meta doc supports literal gate
